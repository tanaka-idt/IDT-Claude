# Cancellation savings benchmarks (Sep 2026)

Assets for the IMTU subscription retention deck: how US subscription apps use retention arguments,
discounts, past savings and projected savings in their cancel flows.

Deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/TYu6Nnr6y7Gg5cEL1PNyHj

## Files

- `logos/`: brand logos from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) (SVG originals plus
  transparent PNG renders). Trademarks belong to their owners.
- `screens/`: app screens cropped from public court exhibits.
  - `uber_one_*.png`: [FTC v. Uber, Second Amended Complaint](https://search.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/Uber-SecondAmendedComplaintwithRedactions.pdf), Exs. 16 to 20 (filed 4 May 2026, screens from 2024)
  - `grubhub_plus_*.png`: [FTC and Illinois v. Grubhub](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2024-12-17-GrubhubComplaint.pdf), Figs. 14 and 15 (Dec 2024)

## Headline findings

- Uber One is the only verified US app showing both past savings ("You saved $192.43 since joining
  Uber One") and projected savings ("You could save $25 each month") at cancel. The FTC is suing over
  the flow, including savings figures that did not subtract the membership fee.
- Discount to stay is common: Grubhub+ ($15 credit), Walmart+ (50% off a year, targeted), LinkedIn
  Premium (50% off 2 months), Adobe (60 days free), Peacock ($2.99 a month for 6 months), NYT.
- Other savings figures are averages or live outside the cancel flow: Sam's Club YTD Total Savings,
  Walmart+ $380/yr, Target Circle 360 $300+/yr, DashPass $4-5 per order.
- Rules: ROSCA, California B&P 17602 (click-to-cancel shown alongside any offer), New York GBL 527-a,
  Minnesota 325G.58 (ask permission before an offer, once per attempt).
